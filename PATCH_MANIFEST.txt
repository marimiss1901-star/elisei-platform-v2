Purpose: SKU 360 product-level change diagnostics.
- Compare the selected period with the immediately preceding period of equal length.
- Show verified deltas for revenue, orders/sales, returns, average selling price, advertising and confirmed economics.
- Rank up to four diagnostic factors by significance and monetary scale.
- Surface one first action instead of recommending simultaneous changes.
- Never invent historical stock from the current snapshot.
- Suppress comparisons when prior sales/finance/advertising coverage is not confirmed.
- Comparison uses already persisted ELISEI data and makes no additional WB API calls.
- Factors are diagnostic correlations, not claims of proven causality.

Regression: 37/37 backend tests passed.
