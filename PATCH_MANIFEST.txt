ELISEI 5.11.5 PATCH MANIFEST
Base: ELISEI 5.11.4
Frontend: 5.11.5
Backend: 2.23.5
El Core: 5.11.5

Purpose: SKU 360 search-source integrity.
- Search texts are persisted only with an explicit WB nmId that belongs to the requested batch.
- Every persisted phrase is stamped with sourceNmID.
- Unbound or out-of-batch rows are dropped and counted in binding diagnostics.
- Substitute/promo SKU rows are excluded from organic search visibility.
- /api/v2/search-report/product/search-texts uses includeSubstitutedSKUs=false and includeSearchTexts=true.
- SKU 360 search joins only by exact nmID; vendorCode/barcode/chrtID fallback is forbidden.
