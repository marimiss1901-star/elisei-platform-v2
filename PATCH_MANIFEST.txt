Purpose: SKU 360 search provenance trust and automatic legacy refresh.
- Historical search rows without sourceNmID/binding version/origin are hidden.
- Fresh product search-text rows are stamped with sourceNmID, searchBindingVersion=3 and searchOrigin=organic_product_search_texts.
- SKU 360 requires exact nmID + verified provenance; no vendor/barcode/chrt fallback for search phrases.
- Old Search Queries state is automatically re-queued once after deployment so trusted data replaces legacy rows.
- Existing real WB rate_limit is preserved; migration does not force requests before next_allowed_at.
- UI explicitly says that legacy unverified phrases are hidden while revalidation runs.
