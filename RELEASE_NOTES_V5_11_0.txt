SKU 360
- Added contextual SKU 360 drawer without a new left-navigation section.
- Opens from Products, Analytics, Stocks, Finance-by-product and Pricing rows.
- Unifies economics, orders/sales/returns, advertising, search visibility, feedback, stock, stock history, pricing and finance movements.

STRICT IDENTITY
- SKU joins are allowed only by nmID, barcode, vendorCode or chrtID.
- Product title is never a join key.
- Extended stream search results are filtered again by exact product identity before they enter SKU 360.
- Finance movements and stock details are re-filtered by the same strict identity policy.

API

- Uses PostgreSQL wb_stream_items for extended rows with compact saved-stream fallback.
